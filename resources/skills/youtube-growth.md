# YouTube Growth & Optimization Guide

This skill provides a systematic framework for growing a YouTube channel from scratch or optimizing an existing one for maximum views and retention.

## 1. Video SEO & Discovery

### Titles
*   **The Hook**: Place the primary keyword within the first 60 characters.
*   **Formatting**: Use `[Primary Keyword] — [Benefit/Hook] ([Modifier])`.
*   **Psychology**: Use curiosity gaps or "negative" titles (e.g., "Stop doing X") which often have higher CTR.

### Descriptions
*   **The Lead**: The first 150 characters are indexed for search. Front-load keywords here.
*   **Structure**: 150-300 words. Include timestamps (at least 3 chapters) to help Google Search index specific segments.

### Tags & Metadata
*   Use 5-15 relevant tags. Start with the specific primary keyword, followed by broad categories.

## 2. Visual Optimization (CTR)

### Thumbnails
*   **Specs**: 1280x720px (min), 16:9 ratio.
*   **Contrast**: High saturation and brightness to stand out against the white/dark YouTube UI.
*   **Faces**: If using faces, ensure they are "expressive" (shock, joy, curiosity). Use close-ups.
*   **Text**: 3-5 bold words max. Use heavy outlines or drop shadows for legibility on mobile (120px wide).
*   **Dead Zones**: Avoid placing text in the bottom-right (timestamp overlay) or extreme edges.

## 3. Audience Retention (AVD)

### The Hook (First 30 Seconds)
*   **Visual Hook**: Change the scene or add graphics every 3-5 seconds in the intro.
*   **Verbal Hook**: State the problem immediately and preview the "payoff" or result.

### Content Mix
*   **Tutorials (50%)**: Search-based traffic (evergreen).
*   **Shorts (25%)**: Viral discovery (funneling to long-form).
*   **Trend-Jacking (15%)**: High-interest current events.
*   **Community (10%)**: Story posts and updates to keep fans engaged.

## 4. Growth Workflow
1.  **Ideation**: Search for high-volume, low-competition keywords via TubeBuddy/VidIQ patterns.
2.  **Thumbnail-First**: Design the thumbnail and title *before* filming. If you can't make a compelling thumbnail, don't film the video.
3.  **The 48-Hour Blast**: Engage with every comment in the first 48 hours to signal engagement to the algorithm.
4.  **Looping**: Ensure Shorts are "loop-friendly" to increase re-watch stats.
